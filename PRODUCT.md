## 1. Product Overview

### What is GS 360?

GS 360 is a **physical-digital hybrid UPSC preparation platform** that combines:
- AI-powered adaptive study planning — unique plan per student, rewrites weekly
- 24/7 AI voice companion for doubts, quizzes, and interview practice
- AI note generation & content intelligence
- Handwritten answer scanning & AI scoring
- Spaced repetition revision engine
- Physical welcome kits (30+ books) & monthly test packs shipped to door
- Human mentor system with biweekly calls
- Community pods, study buddies & leaderboards
- 500+ hours of SRIAS video lectures

It tracks what students **actually know** vs what they **think they know** — and builds a personalized, adaptive prep journey around real performance data.

### Why GS 360 Wins

| What Others Do | What GS 360 Does |
|---|---|
| Fixed syllabus calendar (same for all students) | **Unique plan per student** based on 9+ variables |
| Plan never changes after purchase | Plan **rewrites itself every Sunday** based on weekly performance |
| No connection between scores and schedule | Low scores **auto-trigger extra sessions** on weak subjects |
| "Revise at your own pace" | System **detects forgetting** and forces targeted recall tests |
| Digital-only content | **30+ physical books on Day 1** + monthly test packs shipped |
| AI decides when you're "done" with a topic | **Mastery-based phase transitions** — fast learners advance early, slow learners get more foundation time |

### One-Stop Solution

```
A student should NEVER need to open another app, buy another book,
or search YouTube for anything UPSC-related.
If they need it, GS 360 has it.

The only things a student still needs:
  - A quiet place to study
  - A smartphone + internet
  - Discipline (we help with that too)
```

## 3. Readiness Test & Onboarding

### Free Readiness Test (no signup required)

A quick diagnostic that tells students exactly where they stand — before they commit to anything.

- **Prelims Check** — MCQs across all GS papers (Polity, Economy, History, Geography, Environment, S&T). The system identifies which subjects the student is strong in and which need serious work.

- **Mains Readiness Check** — Student uploads a handwritten answer or types one. AI evaluates it on structure, analytical depth, content accuracy, value addition, and word economy — the same dimensions UPSC examiners look for.

- **Instant Report** — A detailed breakdown including:
  - Paper-wise heatmap showing strengths and gaps
  - Gap analysis with specific weak areas highlighted
  - UPSC Twin match — matched to a topper who had a similar starting profile
  - Overall readiness score with subject-wise split
  - Shareable score card (Instagram/WhatsApp ready) + downloadable PDF

### Day 0–7 Onboarding

> The first 7 days create "aha moments" that prove the system works.

| Day | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **Day 0** | Welcome video → Profile setup → AI generates personalized 30-day plan |
| **Day 1** | First Command Center — 3 light tasks, strongest subject first |
| **Day 2–3** | Build rhythm: topics + answers + revision. Study buddy matched. |
| **Day 4–5** | Performance heatmap appears. Leaderboard unlocks. |
| **Day 6** | Mini assessment — compare to Day 1 scores |
| **Day 7** | Full progress summary — readiness growth visible |

## 5. Smart Study Plan Engine

### How the AI Builds Your Plan

The plan uses 9+ signals to create a unique study path:

- **Target year** — determines total available months
- **Daily hours** — sets content volume per day
- **Prep level** — starting difficulty calibration
- **Optional subject** — GS overlap mapping for time savings
- **Work status** — lighter weekdays for working professionals
- **Strengths & weaknesses** — more sessions on weak areas
- **Previous attempts** — Mains-intensive for repeaters
- **Schedule preference** — morning, night, or split sessions
- **Readiness Test data** — baseline calibration

### 4-Phase Structure

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Foundation** | High-priority weak topics, basic concepts, 1 answer/day |
| **Integration** | Cross-connections, PYQ practice, current affairs integration, 1–2 answers/day |
| **Mastery** | Full revision cycle, sectional mocks, 2 answers/day |
| **Exam Mode** | Revision + full-length mocks, 2–3 answers/day, weak area drills |

### Weekly Adaptation

The plan **rewrites every Sunday** based on:
- Weak subject boost — low scores auto-trigger extra sessions
- Mastery-based phase transition — strong students advance early
- Topic re-entry — forgotten topics get re-inserted with recall tests
- Difficulty calibration — consistently high scores upgrade to harder content
- Current affairs integration — major news auto-linked to weak topics
- Burnout detection — session drops trigger a lighter Recovery Week

### Personalization Examples

| Student Profile | Plan Generated |
|---|---|
| **Working professional** (4 hrs/day, 2nd attempt, weak Economy) | Lighter weekdays, "Weekend Warrior" mode |
| **Full-time aspirant** (8 hrs/day, 1st attempt, beginner) | Intensive 12-month plan, 3 topics/day + 2 answers |
| **Repeater** (3rd attempt, weak Mains) | Mains-intensive, 3 answers/day, weekly mentor review |

## 10. Answer Writing & AI Scoring

### How It Works

1. **Submit** — Scan handwritten answer (mobile camera with auto-crop/enhance) or type
2. **OCR** — Image → text conversion
3. **AI Analysis** — 5 scoring dimensions analyzed
4. **Score** — Weighted score + 3 improvement suggestions + model answer comparison
5. **Deliver** — Full breakdown + 30-day trend graph — all within 2 minutes

### Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | What AI Checks |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | Intro thesis, paragraph flow, conclusion |
| **Content Accuracy** | Key concepts, factual errors, constitutional articles |
| **Analytical Depth** | Multiple perspectives, cause-effect, critical evaluation |
| **Value Addition** | Diagrams, data points, case studies, committee references |
| **Compliance** | Word count and time taken vs limits |

### Human Review
- Mentor reviews answers with line-by-line feedback and video walkthrough
- AI scoring continuously calibrated against human scoring

## 12. Revision Engine

Based on the **Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve**, each topic follows an adaptive recall schedule:

| Interval | Format |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | 5 MCQs — quick recall check |
| **Day 7** | MCQs + 1 short answer |
| **Day 15** | MCQs + short answer + diagram |
| **Day 30** | MCQs + full answer writing |
| **Day 90** | Light recall check |

**Adaptive logic:**
- Easy recalls → spacing increases (7 → 15 → 30 → 90 → 180 days)
- Forgotten topics → spacing decreases, flagged as "weak" on dashboard
- Max 3 revision tests/day to prevent fatigue
- Sundays are revision-only (no new topics)
