GS360 is the first **open-source, agent-native, AI-powered UPSC learning platform**. It transforms raw study materials into an interactive, cited, and accurate learning experience — for free.

## Why GS360?

In India, UPSC coaching is a multi-crore industry that often prices out the most deserving candidates. GS360 breaks this barrier with:

- **Agent-Native Tutoring** — Powered by a custom-patched DeepTutor engine with 16k+ lines of agent logic. It doesn't just "chat"; it teaches, remembers progress, identifies weak spots, and provides answers grounded in your content.
- **Plug-and-Play Content Packs** — Anyone can create a Content Pack (NCERTs, Laxmikanth summaries, PYQ banks) by organizing files into folders. The system auto-ingests them into isolated vector indices.
- **Hardened RAG Accuracy** — Every week, the engine is tested against a Golden Dataset of 200+ verified UPSC questions. Zero hallucinations about constitutional or factual data.
- **Security-First Multi-Tenancy** — Index isolation, path sanitization, and audit logging protect student data.
